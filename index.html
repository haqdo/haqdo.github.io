Ha Do

I am an astrophysics student at the University of Chicago, where I study stellar streams, chemical abundances, and the origins of the universe in the darkest corners of our galaxy. My work spans r-process nucleosynthesis in dwarf galaxies, historic glass plate digitisation, and SETI target selection. I also study Race, Diaspora, and Indigeneity — because the stars are only one way of thinking about deep time.
